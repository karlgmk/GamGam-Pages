Block Pop - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Block Pop is a mobile game published by GamGam. We aim to keep the game simple and minimise the personal information used by the app.

Information stored by Block Pop

Block Pop does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address or other contact details to play.

Game information such as your high score and information used to manage the timing of advertising is stored locally on your device. GamGam does not operate a user account or cloud gameplay database for Block Pop.

Advertising

Block Pop uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process information in connection with serving, measuring and improving ads. Depending on your device, region, settings and consent choices, this may include information such as your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, advertising data, interactions with ads or the app, and diagnostic or performance information.

Advertising services are provided by Google subject to Google's own privacy practices. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent and advertising choices

Where required, including for users in the UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland, Block Pop uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent choices. Where Google requires privacy options to remain available, Block Pop provides an in-app option to review those choices.

On iOS, Block Pop may also ask for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before allowing access to the device's advertising identifier for tracking. You can deny this permission and still play Block Pop. You can also change tracking permissions later in your iOS settings.

How information is used

Information processed through the advertising service may be used to provide and measure advertising, prevent fraud, maintain and improve advertising services, and, where you have provided the necessary permission or consent, provide more relevant advertising.

Children

Block Pop is a general-audience game and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and privacy settings may be adjusted as required by applicable platform rules and law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Block Pop's features, advertising services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.

Contact

Privacy or support contact details for Block Pop will be added here when GamGam's public support channel is established. Until then, this page remains the current public privacy notice for the app.
